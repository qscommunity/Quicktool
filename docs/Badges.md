# Quicktool Badges

Quicktool badges are lightweight visual indicators that communicate a project's adherence to Quicktool conventions, ecosystem compatibility, and tooling integration.

They are designed to be:

- readable at a glance
- consistent across repositories
- easy to copy-paste
- meaningful beyond aesthetics
